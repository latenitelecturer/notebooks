# Penguin artwork

from https://github.com/allisonhorst/palmerpenguins (`man/figures/lter_penguins.png`), licence CC0 1.0.
The three species were cropped from the one drawing on 2026-10-08; the artist's handle was removed from the
corner because the slides credit her in their caption ("Artwork: Allison Horst").
